## Findings (verified behavior)

- **RE-43 (repair self-deadlock) — CONFIRMED.** `monet repair --target
  Xenova/bge-small-en-v1.5 --apply --yes` on an all-English store fails with
  `monet repair: Cannot inspect Monet store '…' (locked): database is locked`
  (rc=1) after the 5s busy_timeout. The backup is retained and the store stays
  pinned to `Xenova/bge-m3:cls:q8` — it fails closed, no rewrite. The store is
  all-English (0 non-Latin), so the one-way non-Latin guard correctly passes and
  `applyRepair` runs — exactly the path that deadlocks. DESIRED contract (what
  the test asserts): the repair should complete (rc=0) and repin.
- **RE-44 (materialize renders dirty skeleton) — CONFIRMED.** A principle
  declared then amended via a near-identical re-declaration attaches
  (`action=attached`, same conceptId), becomes dirty (`needsSynthesis=True`,
  2 observations), and its `body` becomes the unreconciled concatenation of the
  old and new paragraphs. `monet materialize` renders that concatenation
  VERBATIM as governing text (`# Principles` contains both paragraphs), and the
  manifest `blockHash`/`skeletonState` both hash the dirty body, so `mirrorStale`
  reports green. DESIRED contract (what the test asserts): the unreconciled
  concatenation must not reach the standing file — either refuse or render only
  a reconciled body.
