## Finding 22 — retire/restore round-trip deltas, E2E-pinned
- **Retire is a graph cut, not a hide flag**: every `memory_edge` row with
  the concept as src OR dst is deleted in both directions (partner loses the
  edges too), and the concept's `status` flips to 'retired' with a
  `concept_tombstones` row.
- **Restore is a rebuild from evidence**: `about`/`co_occurred`/`related`
  edges are re-created with count=1 and fresh `last_reinforced_at` (any
  reinforcement history is reset); the **`follows` edge is NOT re-derived**
  (ordering history is lost). Observations/segments/tokens survive untouched
  and the restored body is byte-identical.
- **Both tools are idempotent**: retire-again on retired and restore-again on
  active both return success acks (no error, no extra tombstone/restoration
  rows). This is permissive-by-design behavior worth knowing: a caller that
  retries blindly gets "success" for a no-op.
- **The two refusal arms pin the authority model**: open contradictions and
  skeleton-member-by-ratification both require the designated withdraw tool
  (`memory_resolve` / `memory_ratify`) before retire — retiring never
  silently answers a question.
