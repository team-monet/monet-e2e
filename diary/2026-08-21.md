## Notes / pitfalls

- Confirmed empirically that a **global-breadth** skeleton member is created by
  `memory_declare { circle: "*" }` — the ack reports the derived project circle,
  but `skeleton_breadth` lands global, so a `--global` surface renders it (the
  circle-scoped surface correctly filters it out via `breadth === "local"`).
- materialize manifest/DB paths resolve through `getMonetDir` (MONET_STORAGE_DIR
  first), so MONET_STORAGE_DIR=store + MONET_PROJECT_DIR=base cleanly isolates
  every materialize subprocess (GR-01/07).
- The "store-not-readable" error needs a manifest that already has a surface but
  a bad dbPath — achieved by registering into a fresh dir then making its
  `monet.db` a *directory* (statSync isFile() false).
